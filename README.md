# Funding Opportunity Finder

A Streamlit application that helps Northern Addiction Research Alliance (NARA) members find research funding opportunities related to alcohol, addiction, mental health and connected health topics.

The app collects opportunities from:

- British Academy
- National Institute for Health and Care Research (NIHR)
- Wellcome
- UK Research and Innovation (UKRI)
- Society for the Study of Addiction (SSA)

Users can search and filter opportunities, view funding details and download results.

## How to log in

**This funding finder is restricted to authorised NARA member access.**

1. Open the application.
2. Enter **NARA-CAMHR@hull.ac.uk** as the email address.
3. Enter the password provided to authorised members.
4. Click **Log in**.

Once logged in, select your funding sources and click **Fetch funding opportunities**. Use the filters to refine your results, and select **Sign out** when finished.

Always check the original funder’s website to confirm eligibility, deadlines and application requirements.
